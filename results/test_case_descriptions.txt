GIAI THICH THUAT NGU

- I2T (Image-to-Text): Thanh phan loss so sanh dac trung anh voi dac trung van ban cua cac lop/danh tinh.
- K: So luong lop am (lop khong phai nhan dung) duoc dua vao tinh loss; lop dung van duoc giu lai lam lop duong.
- random_k: Voi moi mau, lay ngau nhien K lop am de so sanh voi lop dung. Trong cau hinh mac dinh, K = 64 (hoac giam neu so lop am it hon).
- top_k: Chon K lop am co diem cao nhat de mo hinh tap trung phan biet cac lop am kho; cau hinh mac dinh K = 64. O thi nghiem top_k rieng, viec chon loc xet tren cac lop; o top_k_random_pool, top K duoc chon trong pool ngau nhien da lay.
- random_pool: Lay ngau nhien toi da 256 lop am lam pool ung vien chung cho batch (bo qua cac lop duong cua batch), sau do tinh loss tren cac ung vien nay va lop duong.
- top_k_random_pool: Bat dong thoi top_k va random_pool; code runner bat top_k va random_pool, tat random_k. top_k_random_pool la ten nhom thi nghiem, khong phai mot lua chon K-doc lap.
- baseline: Khong bat top_k, random_k hoac random_pool; loss Image-to-Text tinh tren toan bo cac lop.
